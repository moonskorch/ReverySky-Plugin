# What's New Since Launch

Since ReverySky 3D Graph was first released, several new features and improvements have landed. Here are some of the highlights.

## Ego Graph

Explore the local neighborhood around the active note with adjustable depth. The graph follows your current context as you move between notes or select nodes. Switch to another note in the editor or click a node in the graph, and Ego Graph rebuilds around it.

Settings:

* `Ego mode`: turns Ego Graph on or off.
* `Depth`: controls how many link steps from the center are included, from 1 to 5. Both incoming and outgoing note links count toward the neighborhood.
* `Neighbor links`: shows all links between notes included in the Ego Graph. When disabled, only links connecting one layer of the neighborhood to the next remain visible.

Your current filters, tag visibility, and layout settings continue to apply to the resulting graph.

## Simplified Chinese support

Note titles and tags can now display Simplified Chinese characters.

## Graph screenshots

Capture and copy a screenshot of your current 3D graph directly from the graph view. Use the `Copy screenshot` command, or click `Copy screenshot` in the Settings panel. The screenshot is copied to your clipboard as a PNG image.

## Performance & polish

Graph rebuilds and camera-facing elements have been optimized, with additional controls for frame rate and visual quality. Filters, settings, navigation, and other parts of the interface have also received a number of improvements.
