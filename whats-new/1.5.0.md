# What's New in 1.5.0

## Landmarks

Landmarks let you take keywords or key phrases from a note and display them directly in the graph. The metaphor here is: notes are planets, and Landmarks are points of interest on them. Names, places, events, concepts, project names, and other important details can stay visible while exploring connections instead of being hidden inside the note.

### Adding a landmark

1. Open a Markdown note.
2. Select a word or phrase.
3. Right-click the selection.
4. Choose **Add landmark**.

If the graph is already open, it updates immediately and switches to Landmarks view so the new landmark appears right away.

Use the round view button to switch views without changing the layout: the same spatial structure can be viewed through connections or through Landmarks.

Landmarks are stored in the note's `landmarks` frontmatter property, where they can also be edited or removed.
