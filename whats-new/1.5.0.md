# What's New in 1.5.0

## Landmarks

Landmarks let you take keywords or key phrases from inside a note and display them directly on the graph as labels around its node. Names, places, events, concepts, project names, and other important details can stay visible while exploring connections, instead of being hidden inside the note.

### Adding a landmark

1. Open a Markdown note.
2. Select a word or phrase.
3. Right-click the selection.
4. Choose **Add landmark**.

If the graph is already open, it updates immediately and switches to Landmarks view so the new landmark appears right away.

Use the round view button in the graph to switch to Landmarks view at any time.

Landmarks are stored in the note's `landmarks` frontmatter property, where they can also be edited or removed.
